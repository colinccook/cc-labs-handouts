## Problem
The "💰 Token-smart" notes give good advice, but `/context` is the only thing learners actually measure. They never see real spend.

## Why it matters
Advice sticks when people can see numbers. Comparing a full loop pass with the "just build it" anti-demo (Ex 2 bonus) would **prove** the course's cost argument rather than assert it — and gives teams evidence when making the case for budgets.

## Proposed change
- Introduce the cost/usage command in Ex 1.
- Optional: a statusline showing context %, model and cost.
- Ex 8: record the cost of a full pass as part of "observe as you go".

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Both tracks show how to view spend.
- [ ] Ex 8's observe list includes cost.
